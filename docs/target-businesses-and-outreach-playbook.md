## 4. Cold Calling Script (For SDRs & Tele-sales)

### Opening & Pattern Interrupt (First 15 Seconds)
> **SDR:** *"Hi {{First_Name}}, this is [Your Name] from WACRM. I know I caught you out of the blue — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I called, and you can tell me if it's relevant for {{Company_Name}}?"*
>
> *(Customer: "Sure, go ahead / What is this about?")*

### Pitching the Problem (30 Seconds)
> **SDR:** *"Thanks, {{First_Name}}. We work with businesses in {{Industry}} that generate customer inquiries through their website and ads. Right now, most companies have their team chatting with customers on personal WhatsApp numbers, meaning management has no visibility on lead leakages, response times are slow, and past leads never get followed up.
>
> We provide a single verified WhatsApp CRM system where your entire team answers from one central dashboard, with AI handling after-hours inquiries, and automated broadcasts recovering lost quotes."*

### The Hook Question
> **SDR:** *"Are your sales reps currently handling customer WhatsApp chats from their personal mobile phones, or do you have a centralized system in place?"*

### Handling Common Objections
- **Objection 1: "We already use WhatsApp Business app on a mobile phone."**
  - *Response:* *"That works when you have 1 person. But as soon as you have 2+ agents, you can't assign chats, track who responded, prevent contact data theft when an employee leaves, or send automated ERP invoice notifications. WACRM gives your whole team shared access on desktop and mobile."*
- **Objection 2: "We use email or standard CRM like Salesforce/HubSpot."**
  - *Response:* *"We don't replace your CRM — we make it accessible on WhatsApp. Today, emails get an 18% open rate while WhatsApp gets 98%. WACRM connects directly into your existing CRM or ERP so notifications and quotes go straight to the customer's phone."*
- **Objection 3: "Send me an email."**
  - *Response:* *"I'll gladly send that right over. Can I send it directly to your WhatsApp with a 1-minute overview video so you can review it on the go? What's your best number?"*

### The Close for the Meeting
> **SDR:** *"Let's do this: I can show you a live 10-minute preview tailored to {{Company_Name}}'s exact sales flow. How does Wednesday at 11:30 AM or Thursday at 3:00 PM look on your calendar?"*
